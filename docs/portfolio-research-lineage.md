Status vocabulary:

- **Implemented artifact** — inspectable code/repository exists.
- **Research direction** — concept/hypothesis/title discussed; not automatically a paper.

## 6. Publication integrity rule

A repository can truthfully support phrases such as **implemented**, **tested**, **synthetic benchmark**, or **research prototype** when its code and CI support those claims.

Do **not** upgrade that language to:

- published research;
- production deployment;
- real customs/hospital/port use;
- validated hardware;
- real-world safety guarantee;
- measured scanner accuracy;

unless independent evidence exists.

A candidate title should move to an arXiv/preprint manuscript only after the relevant repository has a frozen research question, reproducible experiment, baselines, results, error/uncertainty analysis, negative findings, limitations and a clearly documented authorship trail.
