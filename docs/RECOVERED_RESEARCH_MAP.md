## Publication rule
A title moves toward an arXiv/Zenodo preprint only after:
1. a concrete research question is frozen;
2. an implementation or dataset exists;
3. baselines are defined;
4. experiments are run;
5. results and negative findings are recorded;
6. the manuscript clearly distinguishes proposals from measured evidence.

This prevents a project idea from being misrepresented as completed research.
