# Research agenda

## Central question
**Can a compact evidence-and-permission governance layer make multi-agent autonomy more inspectable without requiring a monolithic agent framework?**

## Hypotheses
### H1
Explicit permissions prevent unauthorized low-risk actions even when sufficient evidence exists.

### H2
Evidence requirements reduce premature execution for medium/high-risk tasks.

### H3
Critical-risk escalation provides a stable hard boundary independent of model confidence.

## Experiments
1. Enumerate the permission × risk × evidence state space and verify invariant behavior.
2. Inject conflicting agent roles and measure whether task-specific permissions prevent privilege bleed.
3. Extend the prototype with memory-governor and evidence-probes; measure composed decision consistency.

## Metrics
- unauthorized ACT rate
- premature ACT rate
- escalation rate by risk
- audit completeness
- policy invariant violations
